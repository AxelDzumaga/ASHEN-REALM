# Stage 60 — Canonical art prompt set

Estos prompts son una guía de regeneración coherente, no una certificación de arte final.

## Bloque común

`cartoon-chibi dark fantasy mobile RPG character, 3/4 side combat view, large head and compact body, slightly exaggerated hands and weapon, clean dark outline, soft 2-3 level cel shading, warm ember key light, restrained rim light, medium detail, strong readable silhouette, transparent background, centered lower pivot, consistent camera and perspective, no text, no UI, no scenery, no painted ground shadow`

## Ashen Wanderer

`hooded ash wanderer hero, asymmetrical charcoal cloak, ember eyes, burnt metal light armor, broad readable ember sword, red scarf accents, confident combat stance, compact heroic chibi proportions`

## Ash Crawler

`fast ash crawler enemy, low insectoid silhouette, hooked forelimbs, teal ash markings, nervous crouched combat pose, narrow readable mass, ember fissures`

## Ember Wretch

`mid-sized ember wretch enemy, hunched broad torso, hooked sickle, violet-red cloth accents, aggressive forward stance, distinct medium silhouette`

## Ashbound Brute

`heavy ashbound brute enemy, wide armored shoulders, oversized shield and gauntlet, turquoise secondary metal, planted tank stance, massive compact silhouette`

## Ashen Warden

`premium Ashen Warden boss, very large armored sentinel, oversized ember greatblade and glowing gauntlet, crown-like ash helm, red mantle, dominant deliberate stance, clear boss silhouette`

## Negative constraints

`no photorealism, no 3D render, no pixel art, no realistic anatomy, no tiny microdetail, no muddy black-orange wash, no background, no floor, no shadow blob, no logo, no lettering, no extra limbs, no alternate camera angle`

## Animación

Generar hojas separadas por personaje y estado cuando exista capacidad consistente: `idle`, `attack`/`basic_attack`, `ember_slash` o especial, `hit`, `guard`/`phase_transition`, `death`. Mantener el mismo encuadre y padding en todos los frames. Las hojas actuales 59B y las animaciones previas se consideran **PROVISIONALES** hasta revisión humana.
